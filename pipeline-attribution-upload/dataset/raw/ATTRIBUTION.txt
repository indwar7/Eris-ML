Chronicling America / National Digital Newspaper Program (NDNP)
Library of Congress
Source: https://chroniclingamerica.loc.gov/

Data fetched from the public bulk-data mirror at
https://chroniclingamerica.loc.gov/data/batches/ on 2026-08-12.

All text in snippets.jsonl is raw OCR output (ALTO XML <String CONTENT="...">
values, concatenated) produced by the digitization vendor's OCR engine for
each real NDNP batch, as recorded in that batch's own ALTO metadata
(softwareCreator / softwareName / processingAgency). Nothing has been
cleaned, corrected, or paraphrased.

The Library of Congress's rights statement for Chronicling America is that
the newspapers in the collection "are in the public domain or have no known
copyright restrictions." Newspapers published in the United States more than
95 years ago are in the public domain in their entirety; those published less
than 95 years ago are also believed to be in the public domain but may contain
some copyrighted third-party material. NDNP admits only newspaper titles/runs
that the contributing state institution has independently confirmed
rights-clear for digitization and public redistribution -- that is the basis
on which the Library publishes this content at all.

Sampled issue dates in this fetch span roughly 1831-1960. Per the Library's
own advisory, users of snippets with an issue_date within the last 95 years
should be alert for modern third-party content that may carry copyright;
batches_meta.csv / snippets.jsonl record issue_date per snippet for exactly
this purpose.
